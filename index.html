PRINCIPAL INVESTIGATOR

MEET THE PI

Junqiang Zhao, PhD

Assistant Professor · Cancer Control
Department of Surgery, University of Rochester

Dr. Zhao studies how research can inform care and how people can participate in decisions about their health. His work brings together implementation science, behavioral perspectives, and realist and mixed methods.

He joined Rochester in July 2025, following roles at the University of Toronto and Waypoint Research Institute. He earned his doctorate in nursing at the University of Ottawa.
