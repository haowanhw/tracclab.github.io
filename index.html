TRACC Lab

Translating research into action in cancer care.

The TRACC Lab advances the implementation of evidence-based cancer screening and supportive care in real-world clinical settings. Our goal is to help ensure that effective cancer care reaches the people and communities who need it.

Explore our researchMeet the team

What we bring

Research expertise

Implementation science

  • Select and apply implementation theories, models, and frameworks
  • Design implementation studies, including trials
  • Identify and assess multilevel implementation barriers and facilitators
  • Develop and adapt implementation strategies
  • Identify and evaluate implementation mechanisms
  • Define and measure implementation outcomes
  • Evaluate implementation sustainability and scale-up

Patient decision support

  • Assess patient decisional needs
  • Develop patient decision aids
  • Evaluate decision support outcomes

Research methods

  • Realist inquiry
  • Qualitative studies
  • Mixed methods
  • Systematic review
