Principal Investigator

Junqiang Zhao, PhD

Dr. Junqiang Zhao is an Assistant Professor in the Supportive Care in Cancer Division, Department of Surgery at the University of Rochester Medical Center and an Associate Researcher at the Wilmot Cancer Institute. He also holds a status appointment as an Assistant Professor at the Dalla Lana School of Public Health, University of Toronto.

Dr. Zhao’s research expertise spans implementation science and shared decision-making. He currently serves as Vice Chair of the Contextual Drivers Action Group within the Consortium for Cancer Implementation Science. He is also an Editorial Board Member for Implementation Science Communications and a Junior Editorial Board Member for the Asian Pacific Journal of Oncology Nursing. He leads the TRACC (Translating Research into Action in Cancer Care) team, which focuses on applying implementation science to improve cancer screening and supportive cancer care. Methodologically, his research specializes in realist methodology and mixed-methods research.

Prior to joining the University of Rochester in 2025, Dr. Zhao was an Implementation Scientist at the Waypoint Research Institute. He earned his PhD in Nursing from the University of Ottawa, where he received systematic training in implementation science at the Centre for Implementation Research, Ottawa Hospital Research Institute.

Research Assistant · PhD Candidate

Research Assistant · PhD Candidate

Zijun Xie

Zijun Xie is a PhD candidate in the Epidemiology program at the University of Rochester Medical Center. She received her Master of Science in Epidemiology from Johns Hopkins University in 2025. Her research primarily focuses on environmental exposures, particularly chemical exposures. Her current work also explores how health policies and artificial intelligence (AI) models influence cancer treatment and care delivery in real-world clinical settings.

Research Assistant · PhD Candidate

Yutong Xu

Yutong Xu is a PhD candidate in Epidemiology at the University of Rochester. She holds a master’s degree in Biostatistics and a bachelor’s degree in Mathematics. Her research interests include cancer research, implementation science, infectious diseases, and health disparities, with a focus on applying quantitative methods to understand and improve health outcomes. Her previous research experience includes oncology clinical trials, cancer screening implementation, and health disparities among underserved populations.

Research Assistant · MS Candidate

Jingqiu Zhang

Jingqiu Zhang is currently an MS candidate in the Health Services Research and Policy program. She is a mixed-methods public health policy researcher with a focus on chronic diseases among older adults. Her recent work centers on cancer prehabilitation, particularly for vulnerable older populations—as well as long-term care research in nursing homes. Before beginning her current master's studies, Jingqiu earned a master's degree from Harbin Medical University and completed a two-year research internship in the Department of Cost and Pricing Research at the China National Health Development Research Center (CNHDRC). Following her internship, she worked in the public medical insurance department at the First Affiliated Hospital, Zhejiang University School of Medicine, where Jingqiu was responsible for DRG-based data analysis and insurance reviews for cancer medications.
